My Process

How I approach every project

A structured engineering process from problem discovery to deployment.

Step 01

Understand the Business Problem

I start by understanding the real problem — not just the technical requirements, but the business context and user needs behind them.

Step 02

Research & Plan

Thorough research into the best tools, architecture patterns, and approaches. I plan for scalability from day one.

Step 03

Design the Architecture

I design clean, modular architecture with clear separation of concerns — API layer, business logic, data access, and infrastructure.

Step 04

Build API & Frontend

Iterative development with regular feedback loops. I build the backend API first, then connect the frontend — always with testing in mind.

Step 05

Test & Optimize

Rigorous testing — unit, integration, and performance. I optimize database queries, API response times, and frontend bundle sizes.

Step 06

Deploy & Maintain

Deployment with CI/CD pipelines, monitoring, and ongoing maintenance. I ensure the system stays reliable and performant in production.
